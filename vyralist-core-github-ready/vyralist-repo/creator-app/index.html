Getting Started
What is Vyralist?

Vyralist is a platform that connects everyday creators with brands looking for authentic UGC (user-generated content). You get paid to create short videos showcasing products — no huge following required. Brands care about quality content, not follower counts.

Who can join Vyralist?

Anyone 18 or older can apply to join as a creator. You don't need a specific number of followers — we look for creators who can produce clear, engaging video content on a smartphone. If you're comfortable on camera and can follow a creative brief, you're a great fit.

What equipment do I need?

A modern smartphone is all you need. Most top creators on Vyralist shoot with their phones. Good lighting — natural light near a window works great — and a stable surface or inexpensive tripod makes a noticeable difference. Free editing apps like CapCut handle everything else.

How do I complete my profile?

Head to your Profile tab and fill in: your name and photo, your About Me tags (these help brands find you), your shipping address (required for product campaigns), and your PayPal email to receive payments. A complete profile gets matched to more campaigns.

Campaigns & Offers
How do I get matched to campaigns?

Our system matches you to campaigns based on your About Me tags, demographics, niche, and past performance. The more complete and accurate your profile, the better your matches. You'll see a match percentage on each offer card so you know how well you fit before applying.

Can I apply to multiple campaigns at once?

Yes! You can apply to as many campaigns as you like. We recommend focusing on ones that genuinely match your niche and lifestyle — brands can tell when content feels forced. Quality applications beat quantity every time, and a strong match score improves your acceptance rate.

What happens after I apply to an offer?

Once you apply, the brand reviews your profile and pitch video. This typically takes 3–5 business days. If accepted, you'll get a notification and the task will appear in your To-do tab with the full brief, deadline, and video requirements. If not selected, your profile remains active for future campaigns.

Do I keep the product after filming?

Yes — for product campaigns, the product is yours to keep regardless of the outcome. Some campaigns are cash-only (no product shipment), and some include both a product and a cash payment. The offer card clearly shows what's included before you apply.

Creating & Submitting Videos
What makes a great UGC video?

The best UGC videos feel authentic, not scripted. Key tips: film in good natural light, keep your background clean and uncluttered, speak clearly and at a natural pace, show the product being used (not just held), and hook the viewer in the first 2 seconds. Always follow the brand brief closely — it's your roadmap.

How do I submit a video?

Go to your To-do tab, find the active task, and tap "Submit video." You can upload directly from your phone's camera roll. Make sure your file meets the requirements listed in the brief (resolution, length, aspect ratio). Once submitted, the brand receives it immediately for review.

How long does video review take?

Brands typically review submissions within 3–5 business days. If you haven't heard back within 7 days, our team follows up on your behalf automatically. You'll receive a notification the moment your video is approved or if a revision is requested.

What if the brand requests a revision?

If a revision is requested, you'll see detailed feedback in the task. You'll have 48 hours to submit the updated video. Most campaigns include one free revision round. Read the feedback carefully — brands usually give specific notes that make the fix straightforward. Repeated revision requests can affect your rating.

What are the video length and format requirements?

Requirements vary by campaign. Most brands request vertical video (9:16 ratio) at 15, 30, or 60 seconds. Shoot in at least 1080p — modern smartphones handle this by default. The specific requirements are always listed in the campaign brief before you accept the task.

Payments
How do I get paid?

Payments are sent to your PayPal account on the 13th or 26th of each month, whichever comes first after your video is approved. Make sure your PayPal email is saved under Profile → Payment info. You can track your earnings and payment status anytime in your Portfolio tab.

How much can I earn?

Pay per video depends on your creator tier and video length. Regular creators earn $35 for 15-second videos, $45 for 30-second, and $60 for 60-second videos. Premium creators unlock higher rates and exclusive brand deals. Earnings increase as you build a track record of approved, high-rated videos.

When do I get paid if my video needs a revision?

Payment is triggered once the brand gives final approval — whether that's after the initial submission or after a revision. Your earnings will be included in the next payout cycle (13th or 26th of the month) after final approval.

I haven't received my payment — what do I do?

First, confirm your PayPal email is correct under Profile → Payment info. Payments go out on the 13th and 26th of each month — if the next cycle has passed since your approval and you still haven't received payment, tap "Send us a message" in the Help screen and our team will investigate within 24 hours.

Ratings & Tiers
How is my creator rating calculated?

Your rating is based on four factors: video quality (40%), on-time delivery (30%), brand communication (20%), and revision requests (10%). Brands rate each video after approval. Maintaining a strong rating unlocks premium campaigns and higher pay rates.

How do I become a Premium creator?

Premium status is unlocked once you hit three milestones: 14 approved videos, an average rating of 4.8 or above, and a 90%+ on-time delivery rate. Once eligible, the Vyralist team will reach out with an upgrade link — you don't need to do anything. Track your progress under Profile → Creator Tier.

What perks do Premium creators get?

Premium creators get access to higher-paying campaigns that aren't visible to Regular creators, priority matching with top brands, and higher per-video rates. Premium status also signals to brands that you're a reliable, high-quality creator — leading to more direct campaign invites.

Account & Profile
Why should I fill in my About Me tags?

Tags are how brands find you. When a brand sets up a campaign targeting dog owners, fitness enthusiasts, or parents, creators with those tags get matched first. The more accurately your tags describe you, the more relevant campaigns you'll see — and the higher your match score will be.

Can I update my profile information?

Yes, you can update your name, photo, About Me tags, shipping address, and payment details at any time from the Profile tab. Changes take effect immediately. If you update your shipping address mid-campaign, let the brand know through the messaging tab.

How do I request time off?

Go to Profile → My availability and tap "Request time off." This pauses new campaign assignments while you're away. Note that you'll need to complete or resolve any active tasks before going unavailable. Your existing campaigns are unaffected and you can return to active status at any time.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. Once confirmed, a deletion request is sent to our support team who will finalize it within 5–7 business days. All your data, videos, and earnings history will be permanently removed. Any pending payments due to you will still be processed before deletion is completed.
